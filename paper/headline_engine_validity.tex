\title{Asymptotic validity of the within-country decline-certification engine\\
\large A validity statement for \texttt{certify\_decline\_differences}}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

The empirical headlines of the paper---``persistent distribution-wide trust decline is
certified in one country (Greece)'' on the ESS, and the $2.6$--$6.5\times$ over-count on the
World Values Survey---are produced by \texttt{certify\_decline\_differences}, \emph{not} by
the finite-sample-exact clustered conformal band. That engine is a within-country,
design-based, studentized bootstrap procedure, and its guarantee is \emph{asymptotic}. This
note states that guarantee honestly and gives its conditions and proof, so that ``exact''
is never asked to cover the headline.

\section*{Estimand and procedure (as implemented)}

Fix one country. Let $r=0,\dots,L$ index its aligned survey rounds and let
$\theta_r(t)=\Pr_{\text{fin}}(Y\le t)$ be the finite-population CDF of the ordinal response
$Y$ at round $r$, threshold $t$. Because the analysis is \emph{within} a country, the
country effect cancels in the consecutive contrast
\[
  D_{p}(t)\;=\;\theta_{r_{p}+1}(t)-\theta_{r_p}(t),\qquad p=1,\dots,P\ (P=L-1),
\]
so no cross-country transport or exchangeability is invoked. ``Persistent
distribution-wide decline'' is the claim
\[
  H:\quad D_p(t)\ \ge\ 0\quad\text{for all }(p,t)\in \mathcal G,\qquad
  \mathcal G=\{1,\dots,P\}\times\core,
\]
over a fixed \emph{core} threshold set $\core$ on which $\theta_r(t)\in(0,1)$ (the extreme
thresholds, where every CDF pins to $0$ or $1$ and the contrast degenerates, are excluded).

Let $\Fhat_r(t)$ be the design-weighted (H\'ajek) CDF estimator and
$\Dhat_p(t)=\Fhat_{r_p+1}(t)-\Fhat_{r_p}(t)$. A design bootstrap---stratified-PSU
Rao--Wu resampling for the ESS, weights-only multinomial resampling for the WVS, drawn
\emph{independently across rounds}---yields replicates $\Dhat^{*}_{p,b}(t)$, $b=1,\dots,B$,
with bootstrap standard deviation $\sdhat_p(t)$. The engine forms the studentized,
one-sided sup statistic and its bootstrap quantile,
\[
  W^{*}_b=\max_{(p,t)\in\mathcal G}\frac{\Dhat^{*}_{p,b}(t)-\Dhat_p(t)}{\sdhat_p(t)},
  \qquad
  \widehat c=\text{$(1-\alpha)$ quantile of }\{W^{*}_b\}_{b=1}^{B},
\]
and \emph{certifies} $H$ iff the one-sided lower band is nonnegative everywhere,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:certify}
  L_p(t):=\Dhat_p(t)-\widehat c\,\sdhat_p(t)\ \ge\ 0\quad\text{for all }(p,t)\in\mathcal G.
\end{equation}

\section*{Conditions}

\begin{condition}[Independent rounds]\label{c:indep}
The samples drawn in distinct rounds are independent, so $\Dhat_p$ combines two independent
design-based estimators.
\end{condition}

\begin{condition}[Design CLT]\label{c:clt}
As the per-round sample size $n\to\infty$ (with stable stratum/PSU proportions), the
standardized vector $\big(\sqrt n\,(\Dhat_p(t)-D_p(t))\big)_{(p,t)\in\mathcal G}$ converges
in distribution to a mean-zero Gaussian vector $Z$ with covariance $\Sigma$. This is the
standard design-based central limit theorem for weighted CDFs under stratified multistage
sampling with a growing number of PSUs per stratum.
\end{condition}

\begin{condition}[Non-degeneracy]\label{c:nondeg}
On the core grid $\mathcal G$ the limiting correlation matrix of $Z$ is non-degenerate and
$\sigma_p(t):=\lim \sqrt n\,\sdhat_p(t)>0$; equivalently $\sdhat_p(t)$ is bounded away from
$0$ (enforced by the core restriction and the variance floor).
\end{condition}

\begin{condition}[Bootstrap consistency]\label{c:boot}
The design bootstrap is consistent for the sampling law of $(\Dhat_p(t))$ and its
studentized functionals: conditional on the data, $W^{*}$ converges to the same limit as the
studentized centered sup $\max_{\mathcal G}(\Dhat_p(t)-D_p(t))/\sdhat_p(t)$, in probability.
For the ESS this is Rao--Wu stratified-PSU bootstrap consistency; for the WVS it is invoked
under the weights-only with-replacement design model (Condition~\ref{c:wvs}).
\end{condition}

\begin{condition}[WVS design model, when clusters are unavailable]\label{c:wvs}
The WVS ships no PSU/stratum identifiers, so Conditions~\ref{c:clt}--\ref{c:boot} are applied
under the with-replacement weighted-respondent model. Any unmodeled within-cluster
correlation is \emph{not} captured, and the guarantee below is correspondingly approximate
for the WVS.
\end{condition}

\section*{Validity}

\begin{proposition}[Asymptotic one-sided simultaneous validity]\label{prop:engine}
Under Conditions~\ref{c:indep}--\ref{c:boot},
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] the band \eqref{eq:certify} is an asymptotically valid one-sided simultaneous
lower confidence band for the difference surface:
\[
  \lim_{n\to\infty}\Pr\Big(D_p(t)\ \ge\ L_p(t)\ \ \forall (p,t)\in\mathcal G\Big)\;=\;1-\alpha;
\]
\item[(ii)] consequently the decline certification controls its false-certification rate:
if the truth violates persistence---$D_{p_0}(t_0)<0$ for some $(p_0,t_0)$---then
$\limsup_n \Pr(\text{certify }H)\le\alpha$, and at the boundary $D\equiv0$ the asymptotic
type-I error is at most $\alpha$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(i)} By Conditions~\ref{c:indep}--\ref{c:clt} the standardized estimation error over the
finite grid $\mathcal G$ converges to the Gaussian vector $Z$. Condition~\ref{c:nondeg} gives
$\sdhat_p(t)/\sigma_p(t)\to_p 1$ uniformly on $\mathcal G$, so by Slutsky the studentized
centered sup
$T=\max_{\mathcal G}(\Dhat_p(t)-D_p(t))/\sdhat_p(t)$ converges to $\max_{\mathcal G}\widetilde
Z$, where $\widetilde Z$ is the correlation-standardized limit; its distribution function is
continuous and strictly increasing at its $(1-\alpha)$ quantile $c^{*}$ because $\mathcal G$
is finite and the limit is non-degenerate. By Condition~\ref{c:boot} the bootstrap statistic
$W^{*}$ has the same conditional limit, hence $\widehat c\to_p c^{*}$. Therefore
\[
  \Pr\big(D_p(t)\ge L_p(t)\ \forall\mathcal G\big)
  =\Pr(T\le\widehat c)\ \longrightarrow\ \Pr\big(\max_{\mathcal G}\widetilde Z\le c^{*}\big)=1-\alpha .
\]
\emph{(ii)} Certifying $H$ requires $L_{p}(t)\ge0$ for all $(p,t)$. If $D_{p_0}(t_0)<0$ then
certification forces $L_{p_0}(t_0)\ge0>D_{p_0}(t_0)$, i.e.\ the lower band exceeds the truth at
$(p_0,t_0)$; this event is contained in $\{\exists(p,t):D_p(t)<L_p(t)\}$, whose asymptotic
probability is $\le\alpha$ by~(i). The boundary configuration $D\equiv0$ is least favorable
and attains $\le\alpha$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The asymptotics are in respondents, not countries]
The regime here is $n\to\infty$ in \emph{respondents/PSUs per country-round}---thousands per
cell (the code requires $n\ge100$ for the ESS, $n\ge400$ for the WVS)---which is the
favorable regime, in contrast to the small number $K$ of countries that governs the
finite-sample conformal band. The two instruments therefore live on different sample-size
axes and carry different guarantees: the conformal band is finite-$K$ exact for the
\emph{transport} claim (Theorem~3), while this engine is respondent-asymptotic for the
\emph{within-country decline} claim.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Honest scope]
Proposition~\ref{prop:engine} is asymptotic, not finite-sample exact, and for the WVS it is
approximate under Condition~\ref{c:wvs}. The plug-in verdict (point estimate $\ge0$
everywhere, ignoring design uncertainty) over-certifies; the design-aware band is the object
whose false-certification rate is controlled. Reporting both, as the code does, is what makes
the $N\!\to\!M$ reclassification interpretable.
\end{remark}
