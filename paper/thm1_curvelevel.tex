\title{Non-identification and the necessity of design information,\\ at the curve level\\
\large A defensible rewrite of Theorem~1 (\texttt{thm:impossible})}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

The original impossibility result is stated for a \emph{score}-level additive model
$\widetilde R=R+\xi$ with $R\perp\xi$. That structure is not natural: after the sup/absolute
value that defines the score $R=\max_t|E(t)|$, an additive independent decomposition of
$\widetilde R$ need not exist, and $\xi:=\widetilde R-R$ is not generally independent of $R$
(survey-CDF sampling error is heteroskedastic, with pointwise variance $\sim F(t)(1-F(t))$).
This note moves the result to the \emph{curve} level, where the additive model
$\widetilde F=F+S$ \emph{is} the survey sampling model and the independence of the design
noise from the latent curve is the standard measurement-error structure. The score enters
only as a functional of the curves; no additive-independent structure on the scores is
assumed, and arbitrary heteroskedastic noise is allowed.

\section*{Curve-level observation-error model}

Fix a center curve $\mu\in\mathbb R^{T}$ (a symmetric functional of the countries, or an
external prediction). For each country $c$ write the \emph{latent} deviation curve and its
\emph{design noise} as
\[
  E_c \;=\; F_c-\mu\in\mathbb R^{T},\qquad
  S_c \;=\; \widetilde F_c - F_c\in\mathbb R^{T},\qquad
  \widetilde E_c \;=\; \widetilde F_c-\mu \;=\; E_c+S_c,
\]
where $F_c$ is the true (super-population) CDF trajectory and $\widetilde F_c$ the
design-weighted survey estimate. The observed data are the contaminated curves
$\{\widetilde E_c\}$; the target is the latent curve $\Fnew=\mu+\Enew$ of the held-out
country.

\begin{assumption}[Curve-level additive design noise]\label{ass:model}
The latent curves $E_1,\dots,E_K,\Enew$ are exchangeable with law $\law_E$ on $\mathbb R^{T}$;
the design-noise curves $S_1,\dots,S_K,\Snew$ are i.i.d.\ with law $\mathcal S$ and are
independent of the latent curves. The noise law is only known to lie in an admissible class,
$\mathcal S\in\mathfrak S$. No restriction of $\mathcal S$ to homoskedasticity is imposed.
\end{assumption}

\noindent
$S_c\perp E_c$ is the natural two-level structure: given the finite population the sampling
error is generated by an independent design mechanism, so it is independent of \emph{which}
super-population curve was drawn. We take the admissible class to satisfy:

\begin{assumption}[Admissible noise class]\label{ass:class}
\emph{(a)} $\delta_0\in\mathfrak S$: negligible design noise ($S\equiv0$) is admissible.
\emph{(b)} $\mathfrak S$ is closed under one extra convolution by some nondegenerate
symmetric law $\mathcal N$: there is $\mathcal S_0\in\mathfrak S$ with
$\mathcal S_0*\mathcal N\in\mathfrak S$, and $\law_E$ is likewise expressible with and without
the factor $\mathcal N$.
\end{assumption}

Write the sup-score functional $\rho(E)=\normi{E}$ and let
\[
  \widehat q_{\mathrm{plug}} \;=\; \text{the }\big\lceil(1-\alpha)(K+1)\big\rceil\text{-th
  order statistic of } \{\normi{\widetilde E_c}:c=1,\dots,K\},
\]
so that the \emph{plug-in} band $\mu\pm\widehat q_{\mathrm{plug}}$ is the unstudentized
conformal band of Theorem~3 built on the \emph{observed} curves.

\section*{The result}

\begin{theorem}[Non-identification and necessity of design information]\label{thm:impossible}
Under Assumptions~\ref{ass:model}--\ref{ass:class}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(Non-identification.)} The latent law $\law_E$ is not identified from the
observed law $\law_{\widetilde E}=\law_E*\mathcal S$ over the admissible class: there exist
admissible pairs $(\law_E,\mathcal S)\ne(\law_E',\mathcal S')$ with
$\law_E*\mathcal S=\law_E'*\mathcal S'$.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(No uniform improvement over plug-in.)} Let $\widehat\rho$ be the radius of
any band $\mu\pm\widehat\rho$ whose radius is a measurable functional of the observed curves
$\{\widetilde E_c\}$ alone. If the band has latent-target coverage
$\Prob(\Fnew\in\mu\pm\widehat\rho)\ge1-\alpha$ for \emph{every} admissible noise law
$\mathcal S\in\mathfrak S$, then it cannot be a uniform improvement on the plug-in band:
there is no such $\widehat\rho$ with $\widehat\rho\le\widehat q_{\mathrm{plug}}$ everywhere and
$\widehat\rho<\widehat q_{\mathrm{plug}}$ with positive probability. In the degenerate case
$\mathcal S=\delta_0$ the plug-in band is itself the tightest valid band.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(i)} Let $\mathcal N$ be the nondegenerate symmetric law of
Assumption~\ref{ass:class}(b). Put
\[
  \law_E'=\law_E*\mathcal N,\qquad \mathcal S=\mathcal S_0*\mathcal N,\qquad
  \mathcal S'=\mathcal S_0 .
\]
Both pairs are admissible by Assumption~\ref{ass:class}(b), they are distinct
($\law_E'\ne\law_E$ since $\mathcal N$ is nondegenerate), yet
\[
  \law_E*\mathcal S=\law_E*\mathcal S_0*\mathcal N
  =\law_E'*\mathcal S_0=\law_E'*\mathcal S' .
\]
So the two data-generating configurations induce the \emph{same} observed law while differing
in how much of the spread is latent versus noise. Convolution in $\mathbb R^{T}$ is therefore
not injective on the admissible class, and $\law_E$ is not identified.

\emph{(ii)} A band radius $\widehat\rho=\widehat\rho(\{\widetilde E_c\})$ is a functional of the
observed curves, so its joint law with the observed data is the same in any two configurations
sharing the observed law---in particular in the two configurations of part~(i). In the
configuration $(\law_E',\mathcal S')$ the latent curves carry the \emph{extra} factor
$\mathcal N$ (they are strictly more dispersed than in $(\law_E,\mathcal S)$), so a radius that
suffices for the less-dispersed latent target of $(\law_E,\mathcal S)$ under-covers the
more-dispersed latent target of $(\law_E',\mathcal S')$. Validity for \emph{every} admissible
$\mathcal S$ thus forces the radius to accommodate the most-dispersed admissible latent target
compatible with the observed data---attained as $\mathcal S\to\delta_0$, where all observed
spread is latent.

Concretely, take $\mathcal S=\delta_0\in\mathfrak S$ (Assumption~\ref{ass:class}(a)). Then
$\widetilde E_c=E_c$ for all $c$, the observed curves equal the latent curves, and the target
$\Fnew=\mu+\Enew$ satisfies $\Fnew\in\mu\pm\widehat\rho\iff\normi{\Enew}\le\widehat\rho$ with
$\Enew$ exchangeable with the $K$ observed scores. By the exact-level optimality of the
conformal quantile (Theorem~3 and its rank lemma: the order-statistic radius
$\widehat q_{\mathrm{plug}}$ attains exactly the level $\lceil(1-\alpha)(K+1)\rceil/(K+1)$, and
no observed-measurable radius that is $\le\widehat q_{\mathrm{plug}}$ everywhere and strictly
smaller on a positive-probability event retains that level), any uniform improvement
$\widehat\rho$ would give coverage below $1-\alpha$ at $\mathcal S=\delta_0$, contradicting
validity for every admissible $\mathcal S$. Hence no uniform improvement exists, and at
$\mathcal S=\delta_0$ the plug-in band is the tightest valid band.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What is and is not assumed]
The result uses only the curve-level additive model $\widetilde F=F+S$ with $S\perp F$---the
standard survey measurement-error structure---and never the score-level model
$\widetilde R=R+\xi$ with $R\perp\xi$. The noise law $\mathcal S$ may be arbitrarily
heteroskedastic across thresholds (e.g.\ the $F(t)(1-F(t))$ sampling variance); the sup-score
enters only through the plug-in radius. This is the precise, defensible content the paper
needs: \emph{without information on the design-noise law, no band measurable in the observed
survey curves uniformly narrows the latent-trajectory band below the plug-in.}
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Why the deployed pipeline is consistent with this]
Part~(ii) says a uniform improvement requires information about $\mathcal S$ that the observed
curves alone do not supply. The deconvolution branch obtains exactly that information by
\emph{estimating} the design-noise law from the stratified-PSU bootstrap (the estimated-law
guarantee, Theorem~4), and Proposition~1 shows this information is nonetheless unreachable at
survey scale, so the pipeline reduces to the plug-in band on real data. The impossibility is
therefore the scope boundary, not a defect: it is the reason the safe default is the plug-in
conformal band.
\end{remark}
