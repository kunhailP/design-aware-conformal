\section{Simulation and a design-preserving stress test}\label{sec:simulation}

Simulation establishes two facts: attaching the band to the wrong unit collapses
coverage, and the safe selector meets its guarantees on data it has never seen.

\subsection{The wrong unit collapses coverage}\label{sec:wrongunit-sim}

On a ground-truth simulation ($K=30$ countries, $T=6$ thresholds, errors
correlated across rounds and thresholds; Table~\ref{tab:wrongunit}) we ask each
band for \emph{trajectory} coverage---the whole curve-path at once. All three
bands are unstudentized and finite-sample, so only the score's unit varies. The per-round band, marginally valid at the round level, covers the
whole trajectory only $0.82$ at $L=2$ and $0.50$ at $L=8$; the marginal band
collapses faster; the country-trajectory band holds $\approx0.90$ at every $L$,
because the trajectory is a single exchangeable unit. This is the coverage face of
the paper's thesis: the same mechanism drives the certification-count collapse of
Section~\ref{sec:political}. Pre-registered, reported as produced
(\texttt{e28}).

\paragraph{Severity: what each rung can detect.} At ESS-like design noise (threshold
SE $0.012$; sup-$t$ certification, $\alpha=0.10$, four-threshold core; experiment
E32), $80\%$ power at the \emph{net} rung needs a per-pair decline of $0.02$--$0.03$
CDF points over four pairs---realistic secular erosion---while the \emph{persistent}
rung needs $0.06$--$0.08$ per pair, crisis-scale, and its power \emph{falls} as the
trajectory lengthens. Hence non-certification at the persistent rung is weak
evidence of absence; the powered instrument for secular decline is the net rung,
which Section~\ref{sec:political} co-headlines. Real-data injection is flagged for
the archived replication.

\paragraph{Validation, in three disclosed layers.} \emph{(i)}~A sealed holdout
(450 cells: ten DGP families $\times$ $K\in\{25,\dots,320\}$ $\times$ nine $\rho$;
800 reps each) was frozen and run once---but an audit found its scorer evaluated
the S2 studentized band rather than the deployed band, and its findings motivated
the switch to U0 and the Theorem~\ref{thm:safe} architecture, so we label it
development. \emph{(ii)}~A corrected-scorer rerun of the same grid scores the
deployed band verbatim (fresh seed; the original sealed config was not
preserved---disclosed): all $450$ cells floor-compatible, worst $0.882$,
deconvolution active only at $K=320$, low-$\rho$ width $1.005\times$ plain PCB.
\emph{(iii)}~Because both layers had touched the final design, we sealed one
further validation on six \emph{new} DGP families and grids, script hash recorded
before first execution, run once, reported as produced including a disclosed
first-seal generator bug (E33): $120/120$ cells floor-compatible, worst $0.878$,
zero deconvolution activations below $K=94$ as the floor requires. Full tables and
the finite-$K$ mechanism are in the Supplementary Material.

\paragraph{Design-preserving stress test, honestly bounded.} Subsampling the real
AmericasBarometer, the deployed selector never fires; but the pooled $\rhohat$
turnover at deep subsampling coincides with $\approx1$ PSU per stratum, where the
$m$-of-$m$ bootstrap's variance estimate collapses---so the sweep demonstrates a
failure mode of the $\rhohat$ diagnostic under sparse-PSU designs (coverage stayed
at or above nominal throughout), not a saturation law; the regime characterization
of Section~\ref{sec:evidence} rests on the full-sample estimates instead
(Supplementary Material).
