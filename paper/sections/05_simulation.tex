\section{Simulation and a design-preserving stress test}\label{sec:simulation}

Simulation establishes two facts: attaching the band to the wrong unit collapses
coverage, and the safe selector meets its guarantees on data it has never seen.

\subsection{The wrong unit collapses coverage}\label{sec:wrongunit-sim}

On a ground-truth simulation ($K=30$ countries, $T=6$ thresholds, errors
correlated across rounds and thresholds) we ask each band for \emph{trajectory}
coverage, the whole curve-path at once (Figure~\ref{fig:wrongunit}). All three
bands are unstudentized and finite-sample, so only the score's unit varies. A
band that is marginally valid at the round level covers the trajectory $81.7\%$
of the time at $L=2$ and $49.8\%$ at $L=8$; per threshold the figures are
$38.2\%$ and $3.5\%$. Only the country-trajectory band holds its nominal $90\%$
at every length, because the trajectory is a single exchangeable unit. This is
the coverage face of the paper's thesis, and the same mechanism drives the
certification-count collapse of Section~\ref{sec:political}. Pre-registered,
reported as produced (\texttt{e28}).

\paragraph{Severity: what each rung can detect.} At ESS-like design noise (\texttt{e32}),
$80\%$ power at the \emph{net} rung needs a per-pair decline of $0.02$--$0.03$
CDF points---realistic secular erosion---while the \emph{persistent} rung needs
$0.06$--$0.08$, crisis-scale, and its power \emph{falls} as the trajectory
lengthens. Injecting known declines into the \emph{real} ESS pairs, drawing each
estimate from that country's own stratified-PSU bootstrap, puts the thresholds at
$\approx0.033$ (net) and $\approx0.075$ (persistent), with realized size
$0.001$--$0.007$ against a nominal $0.10$: the rule is markedly conservative, and
non-certification is weak evidence of absence at \emph{every} rung (\texttt{e42}). The
powered instrument for secular decline is the net rung, which
Section~\ref{sec:political} co-headlines.

\paragraph{Validation, in three disclosed layers.} A first sealed holdout (450
cells) is labelled \emph{development}, because an audit found its scorer
evaluated the S2 studentized band rather than the deployed one---and its
findings motivated the switch to U0 and the Theorem~\ref{thm:safe} architecture.
A corrected-scorer rerun of the same grid scores the deployed band verbatim: all
$450$ cells floor-compatible, worst $0.882$, deconvolution active only at
$K=320$. Because both layers had touched the final design, we sealed one further
validation on six \emph{new} DGP families, script hash recorded before first
execution and reported as produced including a disclosed first-seal generator
bug (\texttt{e33}): $120/120$ floor-compatible, worst $0.878$, zero activations below
$K=94$ as the floor requires. The audit trail is in the Supplementary Material.

\paragraph{Design-preserving stress test, honestly bounded.} Subsampling the real
AmericasBarometer, the deployed selector never fires, and the pooled $\rhohat$
turnover at deep subsampling coincides with $\approx1$ PSU per stratum, where the
$m$-of-$m$ variance estimate collapses. The sweep therefore demonstrates a
failure mode of the $\rhohat$ diagnostic under sparse-PSU designs, not a
saturation law; Section~\ref{sec:evidence}'s regime characterization rests on
full-sample estimates instead (Supplementary Material).
