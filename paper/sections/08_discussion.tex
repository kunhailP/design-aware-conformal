\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

Uncertainty in claims about repeated cross-national surveys is usually computed for
the wrong unit---twice. We gave a multiplicity-honest simultaneous band for the
claim unit and a hard boundary for the calibration unit: non-identified without the
design-noise law (Theorem~\ref{thm:impossible}), unreachable at cross-national
scale with it (Proposition~\ref{prop:unreach}). The honest deployed object is the
reduction---a clustered band exact at any $K$ inside a selection-free selector
(Theorems~\ref{thm:exact}, \ref{thm:safe})---and the design-aware layer certifies,
rather than corrects, at survey scale.

\paragraph{When it reduces to ordinary conformal.} At the national unit the
method reduces \emph{exactly} to clustered population conformal: the machinery
certifies that the simple band is the honest one and reports the $\rho$ that
justifies it. The deconvolution regime needs many exchangeable units \emph{and}
comparable design noise---never both across countries, but reachable one unit
down (Section~\ref{sec:evidence}) and routine in simulation at many-unit scale
(E31: $K\approx220$--$300$ small areas, deconvolution on up to $98\%$ of draws
at $0.69$--$0.74\times$ the conservative width with valid coverage).

\paragraph{Limitations.} Four scope conditions, stated fully in the Supplementary
Material. \emph{Sampling error only:} the machinery is silent on measurement
non-invariance (wording and translation changes, EVS/WVS house effects, ESS
round-10 mode switches), which produces exactly the shifts the bands certify;
certifications straddling such changes are conditional on measurement constancy.
\emph{Variance-estimator bias:} the deployed $m$-of-$m$ PSU bootstrap
\citep{rao1992recent,wolter2007introduction} biases design variance downward; the
Rao--Wu--Yue rescaled rerun leaves every certification count and gate decision
unchanged (Supplementary Material), though single-PSU strata still carry no
variance information.
\emph{A superpopulation postulate:} transport-band exchangeability is over a
non-sampled, panel-attriting country set (the certifications do not use it), and
holds worse for the post-communist bloc than elsewhere.
\emph{Metadata:} without design metadata (WVS) only weights-only replication is
possible, and the Gaussian-calibrated finite-$K$ remainder is confined by
Theorem~\ref{thm:safe} to the $K\ge94$ regime.

\paragraph{The general lesson.} Attaching uncertainty to the right unit changes
substantive conclusions: persistence in none of thirty-four European democracies
over two decades yet span erosion in eight, mostly invisible to adjacent-wave readings; a
several-fold over-count of persistent deconsolidation, with the surviving core
concentrated outside the consolidated West. The pattern is not confined to
surveys: wherever prediction is calibrated on estimated objects---small-area model
outputs, learned features, meta-analytic effects
(Remark~\ref{rem:general})---the calibration targets carry their own error, and
the claim unit is often coarser than the trend asserted. The right response is not
to widen every band reflexively but to state the strongest object the data
support, correct the estimation noise when it can be identified, and abstain
honestly when it cannot.
