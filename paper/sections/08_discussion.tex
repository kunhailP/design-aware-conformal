\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

Uncertainty in claims about repeated cross-national surveys is usually computed for
the wrong unit---twice. We gave a multiplicity-honest simultaneous band for the
claim unit and a hard boundary for the calibration unit: non-identified without the
design-noise law (Theorem~\ref{thm:impossible}), unreachable for the deployed
correction at cross-national scale with it (Proposition~\ref{prop:unreach}). The deployed object is the
reduction, a clustered band valid at any $K$ inside a selection-free selector
(Theorems~\ref{thm:exact}, \ref{thm:safe}), and the design-aware layer certifies,
rather than corrects, at survey scale.

\paragraph{When it reduces to ordinary conformal.} At the national unit the
method reduces exactly to clustered population conformal: the machinery
certifies that the simple band is the right one here, and reports the $\rho$
that says so. The deconvolution regime needs many exchangeable units and
comparable design noise, never both across countries, but reachable one unit
down (Section~\ref{sec:evidence}) and routine in simulation at many-unit scale
($K\approx220$--$300$ simulated small areas, deconvolution on up to $98\%$ of draws
at $0.69$--$0.74\times$ the conservative width with valid coverage).

\paragraph{Limitations.} Four scope conditions, stated fully in the Supplementary
Material. \emph{Sampling error only:} the machinery is silent on measurement
non-invariance (wording and translation changes, house effects, mode switches),
which produces exactly the shifts the bands certify; certifications straddling
such changes are conditional on measurement constancy.
\emph{Variance-estimator bias:} the deployed $m$-of-$m$ PSU bootstrap
\citep{rao1992recent,wolter2007introduction} biases design variance downward,
though the Rao--Wu--Yue rescaled rerun leaves every count and gate unchanged.
\emph{A superpopulation postulate:} transport-band exchangeability is over a
non-sampled, panel-attriting country set (the certifications do not use it), and
holds worse for the post-communist bloc than elsewhere.
\emph{Metadata:} without design metadata (WVS) only weights-only replication is
possible.

\paragraph{The general lesson.} Attaching uncertainty to the right unit changes
substantive conclusions: persistence in none of thirty-three European democracies
over two decades yet span erosion in eight, mostly invisible to adjacent-wave
readings; a certified deconsolidation set several-fold smaller under a
trajectory-persistence criterion than under wave-pair readings, with the
surviving core concentrated outside the consolidated West. The pattern is not confined to
surveys. Wherever prediction is calibrated on estimated objects---small-area
model outputs, learned features, meta-analytic effects
(Remark~\ref{rem:general})---the calibration targets carry their own error, and
the claim unit is often coarser than the trend asserted. Throughout, absence of certification is
not certification of absence: the severity analysis prices what each claim can
detect, and the counts inherit that price. The right response is not
to widen every band reflexively but to state the strongest object the data
support, correct the estimation noise when it can be identified, and abstain
when it cannot.
