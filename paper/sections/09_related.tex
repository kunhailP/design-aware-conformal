\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

Four literatures intersect here; the Supplementary Material gives the extended
discussion. \emph{Conformal prediction}
\citep{vovk2005algorithmic,lei2018distribution,barber2023conformal}: our selector
adapts not to temporal shift \citep{gibbs2021adaptive} but to the certified magnitude
of survey design noise; within the discipline, \citet{randahl2026bin} calibrate
bin-conditional forecast intervals, a different object from simultaneous trajectory
bands. \emph{Cluster-as-unit conformal}
\citep{dunn2023distribution,lee2023distribution} transports guarantees to an unseen
cluster but treats each cluster's data as cleanly sampled (the same clustered
construction appears, for a non-survey domain, in a companion paper by the present
authors; reference omitted for review); our departure is that the calibration
objects are \emph{estimated} under complex designs
\citep{binder1983variances,lumley2010complex}. \emph{Noisy calibration}: \citet{wieczorek2023design} already brings conformal
prediction to complex survey designs, for unit-level prediction under
design-based inference---our departure is the simultaneous trajectory object, the
population as the exchangeable unit, and the estimated-calibration boundary;
\citet{einbinder2024label,sesia2025adaptive} deconvolve a \emph{known}
label-noise model, an identification premise that generally fails for design
noise (Theorem~\ref{thm:impossible}), and even a supplied noise law faces the
finite-$K$ floor (Proposition~\ref{prop:unreach}). \emph{Functional simultaneous
bands} \citep{diquigiovanni2021conformal}: unlike FDR/Bonferroni corrections of
marginal tests \citep{benjamini1995controlling}, the ``persistent'' object is defined
and covered directly by one band---a distinction of estimand, not error rate
\citep{gelman2012why}. Substantively we engage the trust-decline and
deconsolidation debates \citep{norris2017western,voeten2017people,wuttke2022europeans,
valgardsson2025crisis,foa2016democratic,foa2017signs,ouattara2023distrusting,
vandermeer2025pushing,kolczynska2026trust}; the closest substantive
competitor is \citet{claassen2019estimating,claassen2020support}, whose
latent-variable panels model measurement and sampling uncertainty with model-based
credible intervals---intervals whose calibration is itself contested
\citep{taihusolt2024}---our bands trade pooling and width for finite-sample,
model-free coverage at a stated claim rung, and the two are complements. The certified core's
geography intersects the post-communist disillusionment literature
\citep{mishler1997trajectories,inglehart2003honeymoon}, which anticipated the
decline-from-euphoric-baselines pattern our certification formalizes per country.
