\section{A nominal-safe adaptive method}\label{sec:method}

Theorem~\ref{thm:impossible} says the design bootstrap is the identifying input.
The method turns that input into a band by choosing, target-blind, among three
constructions and by never using the aggressive one unless it is provably reliable
at the available number of countries.

\paragraph{Three branches.} For calibration error curves $\{\widetilde E_c\}_{c=1}^K$
over threshold grid $t$, with per-country design SD $\widehat v_c(t)$ from the
stratified-PSU bootstrap:
\emph{(i) Clustered PCB} takes the conformal quantile of the \emph{unstudentized}
scores $\max_t|\widetilde E_c(t)|$ (the U0 construction of
Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}); it is finite-sample exact for the survey-estimate target at
any $K$, and its latent-target gap is the explicit $O(\rho)$ bound of
Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b). \emph{(ii) Deconvolution} studentizes by the
finite-$K$-safe target scale
$\widehat s_{T}^2(t)=\max\!\big(s_{\text{plug}}^2(t)-[\,\overline{\widehat v^2}(t)-z\,\SE\,]_+,\ \text{floor}\big)$,
which subtracts a \emph{lower} confidence bound on the design variance so the
correction cannot over-shrink; its width scales as $\sqrt{1-\rho^2}$ relative to
PCB. \emph{(iii) Conservative} inflates each unstudentized score by the design SD,
$\max_t(|\widetilde E_c(t)|+z\,\widehat v_c(t))$ --- a score that dominates the PCB
score pointwise, so the conservative band \emph{contains} the PCB band by
construction. This nesting is load-bearing: selection between nested bands anchored
at an exact band is validity-free (Theorem~\ref{thm:safe}), so the selector needs no
conditional-coverage argument.

\paragraph{The three questions.} The naive selector asks only \emph{can we
deconvolve} and \emph{do we need to}. The finite-$K$ failure of deconvolution
(Section~\ref{sec:simulation}) comes from a missing third question: \emph{can we do
it safely at this $K$?} The safe selector adds it explicitly.

\paragraph{Safe selector (Algorithm~\ref{alg:safe}).} Let $\rhohat_{\text{LCB}}$ be
a conservative lower bound on $\rho$, $D=\max_t \SE(\widehat s_T^2)/\widehat s_T^2$ a
reliability diagnostic, and
$\widehat\delta_{\text{UCB}}(D)$ a frozen upper bound on the finite-$K$ coverage
remainder. Deconvolution is used \emph{iff all four gates pass}: (A)
$\rhohat_{\text{LCB}}>\rho_0$ (design noise materially large); (B)
$\widehat\delta_{\text{UCB}}(D)\le\delta_{\max}$ (remainder within budget); (C)
$\widehat{\text{Gain}}-\Delta_g>g_{\min}$ (beats conservative by a margin); (D)
stability. Otherwise the band is PCB when $\rhohat_{\text{LCB}}\le\rho_0$ and the
conservative envelope when design noise is real but information insufficient. Every
gate is a function of the calibration set only, so the selector is target-blind ---
though, by the nested-band argument of Theorem~\ref{thm:safe}, validity would survive
even an adversarial selection between the two anchor branches. The deconvolution
branch, which is \emph{not} nested, is charged its own miss budget
$\alpha_{\text{dec}}=\max(0.1\alpha,3/(K{+}1))$ (the anchors run at
$\alpha-\alpha_{\text{dec}}$), and only in the $K\ge94$ regime where its gate can
open at all; at cross-national-survey $K$ the anchors keep the full $\alpha$ and the
deployed guarantee is exact. All constants are
frozen before validation (Section~\ref{sec:theory}, Appendix~\ref{app:spec}); when
deconvolution is used the reported guarantee is the observable
$1-\alpha-\widehat\delta_{\text{UCB}}(D)$.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Nominal-safe adaptive design-aware band}\label{alg:safe}
\KwIn{calibration curves $\{\widetilde R_c\}$ (unit = country), design replicates
$\{\widehat v_c\}$, target curve $\widehat F_{\text{new}}$, level $\alpha$;
frozen $\rho_0,\delta_{\max},g_{\min},\Delta_g$}
compute $s$, $\widehat s_T$, and the three radii $r_{\text{PCB}},r_{\text{dec}},
r_{\text{con}}$; diagnostics $\rhohat_{\text{LCB}},D,\widehat\delta_{\text{UCB}}(D)$\;
\uIf{$\rhohat_{\text{LCB}}\le\rho_0$}{branch $\leftarrow$ PCB\;}
\uElseIf{$\widehat\delta_{\text{UCB}}(D)\le\delta_{\max}$ \textbf{and} stable
\textbf{and} $\widehat{\text{Gain}}-\Delta_g>g_{\min}$}{branch $\leftarrow$
deconvolution\;}
\Else{branch $\leftarrow$ conservative\;}
\Return $\widehat F_{\text{new}}\pm r_{\text{branch}}$, branch, and diagnostics\;
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Deployment.} The method ships as one call, \texttt{dapcb(cal\_errors,
v\_cal, center, alpha)}, in an installable Python package (module \texttt{pcb}) provided
in the replication package and to be archived with a version tag on the Dataverse,
returning the band together with the selected branch,
$\rhohat$, $\rhohat_{\text{LCB}}$, $D$, $\widehat\delta_{\text{UCB}}$, the coverage
level, and the fallback reason---so a user sees \emph{why} the band was built as it was
and reads off the finite-$K$ guarantee it carries.
