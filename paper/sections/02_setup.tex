\section{Setup and the two targets}\label{sec:setup}

The object we deploy is the exact simultaneous band of Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}; the
impossibility below (Theorem~\ref{thm:impossible}) is a scope boundary delimiting when a
design-aware correction to that band is even available.

\paragraph{Objects.} We observe $K$ source countries, indexed $c=1,\dots,K$, treated
as exchangeable units (the ``population'' or cluster is the exchangeable object, not
the individual respondent). Each country has a latent attitude trajectory
$F_{c,r}(t)$, a CDF over a fixed grid of thresholds $t\in\{t_1,\dots,t_T\}$ and survey
rounds $r$. We do not see $F_{c,r}$; we see a complex-survey estimate
$\Ftilde_{c,r}(t)=F_{c,r}(t)+S_{c,r}(t)$, where $S_{c,r}$ is design (sampling) error
with a design variance $v_{c,r}(t)^2$ that a stratified-PSU / replicate-weight
bootstrap can estimate. Define the transport score of a country as its worst-case
standardized deviation from the transport center,
\[
R_c \;=\; \max_{r,t}\frac{|F_{c,r}(t)-\mu_r(t)|}{\sigma(t)},
\qquad
\widetilde{R}_c \;=\; R_c + \xi_c,
\]
where $\widetilde{R}_c$ is the same quantity computed from the observed $\Ftilde$
and $\xi_c$ is the induced design contamination. (Because $R$ is a supremum, $\xi_c$
is not exactly mean-zero or independent of $R_c$; the additive centered score model
is a working approximation---exact at the curve scale under
Theorem~\ref{thm:estimated}'s scale family---whose failure direction, upward-biased
observed scores, is conservative for the plug-in band.) The governing scalar is the
design-to-\emph{total} SD ratio
\[
\rho \;=\; \frac{\text{SD}(\xi)}{\text{SD}(\widetilde R)}
\;=\;\sqrt{\frac{\overline{v^2}}{s_R^2+\overline{v^2}}}\;\in\;[0,1),
\]
$\rho\to0$ the design-noise-negligible regime, $\rho\to1$ noise-dominated. (The
simulations' generative dial is the unbounded $\mathrm{SD}(\xi)/\mathrm{SD}(R)$;
reported $\rho$ values use the bounded definition.)

\paragraph{Target and validity.} We want a simultaneous band $\widehat B(r,t)$ that
covers a \emph{new} country's \emph{latent} trajectory,
$\Pr\big(F_{\text{new},r}(t)\in\widehat B(r,t)\ \forall r,t\big)\ge 1-\alpha$.
Two facts frame everything. Ordinary clustered conformal on the observed scores is
exact for the \emph{survey-estimate} target $\Ftilde_{\text{new}}$ (it only needs
exchangeability of countries); its coverage of the \emph{latent} target differs by an
explicit gap that vanishes with the design-noise scale
(Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b)---symmetry of the design law alone is \emph{not} enough
for conservativeness, so we bound the gap rather than assume its sign). Meanwhile its
width is inflated by the design noise by a factor growing with $\rho$; correcting
it requires estimating and removing the design contribution---a deconvolution.

\paragraph{Country exchangeability, stated honestly.} The transport band's
identifying assumption is a superpopulation postulate: participating countries'
trajectory laws are symmetric draws---over a set that is not sampled, enters and
exits rounds non-randomly, and changes designs across rounds. A strong
idealization, tempered three ways: the headline certifications of
Section~\ref{sec:political} do not use it (they are within-country design-bootstrap
statements); under approximate exchangeability the \citet{barber2023conformal}
bound controls the coverage gap by an unobserved total-variation distance, treated
as qualitative; a leave-one-region-out audit finds near-nominal held-out coverage
except for the post-communist East ($6/9$ and $5/9$ at nominal $0.90$)---a
quantified scope condition on transport \emph{into} that bloc (Supplementary
Material); and the
Bonferroni rung protects against screening multiplicity---a distinct safeguard
that does not repair non-exchangeability.

\paragraph{The impossibility.} The central obstruction is that the deconvolution is
not identified from the contaminated scores alone.

\begin{theorem}[Non-identification and necessity of design information]
\label{thm:impossible}
Let the observed score be $\widetilde R = R + \xi$ with $R\perp\xi$. (i)~The law of
$R$ is not identified from the law of $\widetilde R$ without restrictions on the law
of $\xi$: there exist distinct pairs $(\mathcal L_R,\mathcal L_\xi)\ne
(\mathcal L_R',\mathcal L_\xi')$ with $\mathcal L_R * \mathcal L_\xi = \mathcal L_{R'}
* \mathcal L_{\xi'}$. (ii)~Consequently, among non-randomized bands that are
measurable functions of the observed scores only, any band whose radius lies
almost surely below the $(\lceil(1-\alpha)(K{+}1)\rceil{-}1)$-th order statistic
of $\{|\widetilde R_c|\}$ has latent coverage at most
$(\lceil(1-\alpha)(K{+}1)\rceil-1)/(K{+}1)<1-\alpha$ on the member $\xi\equiv0$
of the class, which it cannot distinguish from the contaminated one. The only
room below the plug-in radius is the conformal discreteness slack, which
randomized smoothing exhausts; \emph{uniform} improvement beyond it requires
shrinking the class of admissible noise laws.
\end{theorem}

The reading is constructive (proof in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}): a band both honest
and narrower than the plug-in must \emph{supply} the design-noise law---replicate
weights or a design bootstrap, exactly what Section~\ref{sec:method} uses.

\begin{remark}[Beyond surveys]\label{rem:general}
Nothing here is survey-specific: the result holds whenever conformal calibration
uses \emph{estimated} objects---MRP small-area outputs, learned or imputed features,
meta-analytic effects. Design/measurement information is the identifying
ingredient, and its reliability at finite $K$ is itself bounded
(Proposition~\ref{prop:unreach}); confirmed outside surveys on a small-area
calibration (Supplementary Material).
\end{remark}
