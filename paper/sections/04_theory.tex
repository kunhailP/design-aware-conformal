\section{Theory}\label{sec:theory}

Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} anchors the validity architecture and
Theorem~\ref{thm:safe} carries the guarantee through the selector;
Theorems~\ref{thm:oracle} and~\ref{thm:estimated} price what the deployed band
does not get for free --- the latent rather than estimated trajectory, an
estimated rather than known noise law. Proofs are in the Supplementary
Material, Section~S1; each theorem is paired with an automated contract test
checking its computational implications.

\begin{theorem}[Oracle validity]\label{thm:oracle}
Suppose the countries are exchangeable and the design law is known.
(a)~The clustered band is finite-sample valid for the survey-estimate target,
$\Pr(\Ftilde_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge1-\alpha$, using exchangeability only.
(b)~For the latent target the same band satisfies, for every $u>0$,
$\Pr(F_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge1-\alpha-\Pr(\xi<-u)-
\Pr(\widetilde R\in(\widehat q-u,\widehat q\,])$.
(c)~The deconvolution band with the true target scale is valid for the latent
target, and finite-sample exact under the scale-family assumption (A1), with
width a factor $\sqrt{1-\rho^2}+o(1)$ of the clustered band.
\end{theorem}

The gap in~(b) is a noise-tail plus a local anti-concentration term, vanishing
with the design-noise scale at rate $\rho^{2/3}$ under variance-only tails and
$\rho$ up to logarithms under sub-Gaussian noise --- but not by symmetry (a
bimodal counterexample, Supplementary Material): hence we estimate and report
$\rho$ rather than assume it away.

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite-$K$ validity of the deployed base band]\label{thm:exact}
Let the base band use the unstudentized cluster score $R_c=\max_t|E_c(t)|$
($t$ ranging over the stacked round-by-threshold grid), with a center
satisfying the symmetric-construction condition (the Supplementary Material
states the four admissible cases). If the countries are exchangeable, then for
every $K$ the band $\widehat B=\text{center}\pm\widehat q$, with $\widehat q$
the $\lceil(1-\alpha)(K{+}1)\rceil$-th order statistic of $\{R_c\}$, satisfies
$\Pr(\Ftilde_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge\lceil(1-\alpha)(K{+}1)\rceil/(K{+}1)\ge1-\alpha$,
distribution-free, with equality at the stated rank level when the scores are
almost surely distinct.
\end{theorem}

The guarantee is exact for the survey-estimate trajectory T1; the latent
trajectory T2 adds the gap of Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b), bounded through the
reported $\rho$. Exactness at every $K$ is bought by excluding two
asymmetries --- in-sample modulation and grand-mean centers containing their
own unit --- at a width cost of at most $5\%$ on the near-homoskedastic real
curves.

\begin{theorem}[Estimated-law validity]\label{thm:estimated}
Assume a common scale family indexed by the design variance (A1),
anti-concentration of the sup-score law near its upper quantile (A2), and
bounded curves with an unbiased size-$B$ design bootstrap (A3); the appendix
states all three precisely. Then (i)~with oracle scales the deconvolution band
is finite-sample exact for the latent target at every $K$, and (ii)~with
estimated scales it satisfies
$\Pr(F_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge 1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{K,B}$ with
$\varepsilon_{K,B}=O\big(\sqrt{\log(KT)/\min(K,B)}\,\big)$.
\end{theorem}

The remainder's direction is fixed by construction: the finite-$K$-guarded scale
$\widehat s_T^2$ of Section~\ref{sec:method} subtracts a lower confidence bound
on the design variance, so outside an $\alpha_2$-probability event the scale
error widens the band. Some shape restriction is not removable (the
counterexample above); (A1) is one sufficient choice. The miss budget is a
frozen function of $K$ alone: below the reliability floor ($K<94$, the regime
in which the gate cannot open at all, Proposition~\ref{prop:unreach}, covering
every certification sample here) the anchors keep the full $\alpha$ and the
deconvolution budget is zero; at $K\ge94$ a frozen $K$-only rule reserves
$\alpha_{\text{dec}}$ (Supplementary Material).

\begin{theorem}[Safe-adaptive finite-$K$ validity]\label{thm:safe}
Assume the countries are exchangeable and the anchors use
Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}'s centers (or the leave-one-out center with its
$K/(K{-}1)$ inflation, Proposition~S1); for the second clause assume
additionally (A1)--(A3). Deploy Algorithm~\ref{alg:safe} with the
unstudentized clustered and conservative branches, scores $\max_t|E_c(t)|$
and $\max_t(|E_c(t)|+z\widehat v_c(t))$, at level $\alpha_{\text{anchor}}$,
and the deconvolution branch at $\alpha_{\text{dec}}$. Then, with no
condition on the selection rule,
\[
\Pr\big(\Ftilde_{\text{new},r}(t)\in\widehat B(r,t)\ \forall r,t\big)\ge1-\alpha\ \ (K<94),
\qquad
\Pr\big(F_{\text{new},r}(t)\in\widehat B(r,t)\ \forall r,t\big)\ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{K,B}\ \
(K\ge94),
\]
the first clause finite-sample, the second carrying
Theorem~\ref{thm:estimated}'s remainder.
\end{theorem}

The clauses name different targets because they rest on different assumptions:
exchangeability alone delivers T1 exactly, with T2 adding
Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b)'s gap, while (A1)--(A3) deliver the latent target
directly, by an anchor-domination lemma, with the remainder
$\varepsilon_{K,B}$ charged to the deconvolution branch alone --- the
same-order term its leave-one-out centering adds is absorbed into
$\varepsilon_{K,B}$ (the Supplementary Material extends
Theorem~\ref{thm:estimated} to that construction). The proof uses no
conditional-on-selection step: the
conservative score dominates the clustered band's score pointwise, selection
between nested bands anchored at an exact band is validity-free ---
nested-conformal machinery \citep{gupta2022nested,yang2024selection}, with
the contribution of engineering the branches so that it applies --- and a
union bound charges the one non-nested branch its own budget.

Two scope notes. At $K\ge94$ the reported level is a frozen calibrated bound,
not a theorem. And the unsurveyed-target deployment uses a leave-one-out
center, outside Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}'s symmetric constructions --- but not
outside theory: inflating the radius by $K/(K{-}1)$ restores finite-sample
validity at every $K$, distribution-free (Supplementary Material,
Proposition~S1), and the released default ships that inflation, at a width cost
under $3.5\%$; the frozen gates still decide on the uninflated radii, so the
sealed validation carries over a fortiori. (The strictly exact split-fold
alternative is infeasible at survey scale: halving the calibration set below
$\alpha^{-1}{-}1$ units makes the conformal radius infinite.)

\paragraph{Efficiency.} The low-$\rho$ deconvolution-to-clustered width ratio is
$1-\tfrac12\rho^2+O(\rho^4)$, proved; the conservative band contains the clustered one
pointwise; and selection cannot inflate miscoverage
(Theorem~\ref{thm:safe}). The remaining width results, one of them open, are in
the Supplementary Material.
