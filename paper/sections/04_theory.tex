\section{Theory}\label{sec:theory}

Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} anchors the validity architecture (the base band,
exact at every $K$ under its symmetric centers) and
Theorem~\ref{thm:safe} carries the guarantee through the selector, under any
selection rule; Theorems~\ref{thm:oracle} and~\ref{thm:estimated} price what
the deployed band does not get for free --- the latent rather than estimated
trajectory, an estimated rather than known noise law. Proofs are in
the Supplementary Material, Section~S1; each theorem is paired with a machine-checked
contract test.

\begin{theorem}[Oracle validity]\label{thm:oracle}
Suppose the countries are exchangeable and the design law is known.
(a)~The clustered band is finite-sample valid for the survey-estimate target,
$\Pr(\Ftilde_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge1-\alpha$, using exchangeability only.
(b)~For the latent target the same band satisfies, for every $u>0$,
$\Pr(F_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge1-\alpha-\Pr(\xi<-u)-
\Pr(\widetilde R\in(\widehat q-u,\widehat q\,])$.
(c)~The deconvolution band with the true target scale is valid for the latent
target, and finite-sample exact under the scale-family assumption (A1), with
width a factor $\sqrt{1-\rho^2}+o(1)$ of the clustered band.
\end{theorem}

The gap in~(b) is a noise-tail plus a local anti-concentration term, vanishing
with the design-noise scale at rate $\rho^{2/3}$ under variance-only tails and
$\rho$ up to logarithms under sub-Gaussian noise --- but not by symmetry (a
bimodal counterexample in the Supplementary Material), which is why we estimate
and report $\rho$ rather than assume it away.

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite-$K$ validity of the deployed base band]\label{thm:exact}
Let the base band use the unstudentized cluster score $R_c=\max_t|E_c(t)|$, with
a center satisfying the symmetric-construction condition
(the Supplementary Material states the four admissible cases). If the countries
are exchangeable, then for every $K$ the band
$\widehat B=\text{center}\pm\widehat q$, with $\widehat q$ the
$\lceil(1-\alpha)(K{+}1)\rceil$-th order statistic of $\{R_c\}$, satisfies
$\Pr(\Ftilde_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge\lceil(1-\alpha)(K{+}1)\rceil/(K{+}1)\ge1-\alpha$,
distribution-free.
\end{theorem}

The guarantee is exact for the survey-estimate trajectory T1; the latent
trajectory T2 adds the gap of Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b), bounded through the
reported $\rho$. Exactness at every $K$ is bought by excluding two asymmetries a
more adaptive band would introduce, in-sample modulation and grand-mean centers
containing their own unit, at a cost of at most $5\%$ of width on the
near-homoskedastic real curves.

\begin{theorem}[Estimated-law validity]\label{thm:estimated}
Assume a common scale family indexed by the design variance (A1),
anti-concentration of the sup-score law near its upper quantile (A2), and
bounded curves with an unbiased size-$B$ design bootstrap (A3); the appendix
states all three precisely. Then (i)~with oracle scales the deconvolution band
is finite-sample exact for the latent target at every $K$, and (ii)~with
estimated scales it satisfies
$\Pr(F_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge 1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{K,B}$ with
$\varepsilon_{K,B}=O\big(\sqrt{\log(KT)/\min(K,B)}\,\big)$.
\end{theorem}

The remainder's direction is fixed by construction: the finite-$K$-safe scale
$\widehat s_T^2$ of Section~\ref{sec:method} subtracts a lower confidence bound
on the design variance, so outside an $\alpha_2$-probability event the scale
error widens the band. Some shape restriction is not removable (the
counterexample above); (A1) is one sufficient choice.

\paragraph{The deployed pipeline.} The miss budget is a frozen function of $K$
alone: below the reliability floor ($K<94$, the regime in which the gate cannot
open at all, Proposition~\ref{prop:unreach}, and the one covering every
certification sample here including the WVS $\ge2$-wave sets at $K=59$--$77$)
the anchors keep the full $\alpha$ and the deconvolution budget is zero; at
$K\ge94$ the budget
$\alpha_{\text{dec}}=\min\{\max(0.1\alpha,3/(K{+}1)),\alpha/2\}$ is reserved
for it (derivation in the Supplementary Material).

\begin{theorem}[Safe-adaptive finite-$K$ validity]\label{thm:safe}
Deploy Algorithm~\ref{alg:safe} with the unstudentized clustered and conservative
branches, scores $\max_t|E_c(t)|$ and $\max_t(|E_c(t)|+z\widehat v_c(t))$, at
level $\alpha_{\text{anchor}}$, and the deconvolution branch at
$\alpha_{\text{dec}}$. Then, finite-sample and with no condition on the
selection rule,
\[
\Pr\big(\Ftilde_{\text{new},r}(t)\in\widehat B\big)\ge1-\alpha\ \ (K<94),
\qquad
\Pr\big(F_{\text{new},r}(t)\in\widehat B\big)\ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{K,B}\ \
(K\ge94).
\]
\end{theorem}

The clauses name different targets because they rest on different assumptions.
Exchangeability alone delivers T1 exactly, with T2 adding
Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b)'s gap; (A1)--(A3) deliver the latent target directly,
by an anchor-domination lemma, and the remainder $\varepsilon_{K,B}$ they carry
is charged to the deconvolution branch alone.

The proof uses no conditional-on-selection step. A nested-band lemma does the
work: the conservative score dominates the clustered band's score pointwise, and selection
between nested bands anchored at an exact band is validity-free. That is nested-conformal machinery
\citep{gupta2022nested,yang2024selection}; our contribution is to engineer the
branches so that it applies. A union bound then charges the one non-nested
branch its own budget. Two scope notes. At $K\ge94$ the reported level is a frozen calibrated bound, not a
theorem. And the unsurveyed-target deployment uses a leave-one-out center, outside
Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}'s symmetric constructions --- but not outside theory:
the deployed band inflated by $K/(K{-}1)$ is finite-sample valid at every $K$,
distribution-free (Supplementary Material, Proposition~S), and the released
default ships that inflation, so the band Theorem~\ref{thm:safe}'s first
clause certifies is the band the package returns; the frozen gates still
decide on the uninflated radii, so the sealed validation carries over a
fortiori, at a width cost under $3.5\%$. The uninflated radius carries an
explicit remainder $\delta_K=O(1/K)$, the probability mass in a window of
relative width $1/(K{-}1)$ above the radius; a $28$-cell simulation across
four families measures $\delta_K$ at zero ---
no cell below the exact-construction floor beyond Monte Carlo error. The
strictly exact split-fold alternative remains infeasible at survey scale:
halving the calibration set below $\lceil1/\alpha\rceil$ units makes the
conformal radius infinite, exactly at the $K$ this paper works at.

\paragraph{Efficiency.} The low-$\rho$ deconvolution-to-clustered width ratio is
$1-\tfrac12\rho^2+O(\rho^4)$, proved; the conservative band contains the clustered one
pointwise; and selection cannot inflate miscoverage
(Theorem~\ref{thm:safe}). The remaining width results, one of them open, are in
the Supplementary Material.
