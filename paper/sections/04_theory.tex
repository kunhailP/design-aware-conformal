\section{Theory}\label{sec:theory}

We state validity for the oracle, the estimated-law procedure, and the deployed
selector, then efficiency. Proofs are in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}; each theorem has
a machine-checked contract test.

\begin{theorem}[Oracle validity]\label{thm:oracle}
Suppose the countries are exchangeable and the design law is known.
\emph{(a)} The clustered band is finite-sample valid for the survey-estimate target:
$\Pr(\Ftilde_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge1-\alpha$, using exchangeability only.
\emph{(b)} For the latent target the same band satisfies, for every $u>0$,
$\Pr(F_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge1-\alpha-\Pr(\xi<-u)-
\Pr(\widetilde R\in(\widehat q-u,\widehat q\,])$: a noise-tail term plus a local
anti-concentration term, vanishing with the design-noise scale ($\rho^{2/3}$
under variance-only tails, $\rho$ up to logarithms under sub-Gaussian noise).
Symmetric design noise alone does \emph{not} make the band conservative for the
latent target---a bimodal counterexample is in the Supplementary
Material---which is why we report the observed $\rho$.
\emph{(c)} The deconvolution band with the true target scale is valid for the latent
target---finite-sample \emph{exact} under the scale-family assumption (A1) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:estimated}---with width a factor $\sqrt{1-\rho^2}+o(1)$ of the
clustered band.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite-$K$ validity of the deployed base band]\label{thm:exact}
Let the base band use the \emph{unstudentized} cluster score $R_c=\max_t|E_c(t)|$ (no
data-dependent modulation), with a center satisfying the symmetric-construction
condition: fixed, split-fold, per-unit from that unit's own data (the deployed
own-previous-round centers), or a symmetric function of all $K{+}1$ curves
(Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} states the four cases). If the countries are exchangeable,
then for \emph{every} $K$ the band $\widehat B=\text{center}\pm\widehat q$, with
$\widehat q$ the $\lceil(1-\alpha)(K{+}1)\rceil$-th order statistic of $\{R_c\}$,
satisfies
$\Pr(\Ftilde_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge\lceil(1-\alpha)(K{+}1)\rceil/(K{+}1)\ge1-\alpha$,
distribution-free---exact for the \emph{survey-estimate} trajectory (T1); the latent
trajectory (T2) adds the explicit gap of Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b), bounded
through the reported $\rho$. Two asymmetries are excluded by construction---in-sample modulation (an
$O(g/K)$ self-inclusion deficit) and grand-mean centers containing their own
unit (an anti-conservative $O(1/K)$ bias, removed by leave-one-out
centers)---and on the near-homoskedastic real curves the width cost of dropping
the modulation is $\le5\%$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Estimated-law validity]\label{thm:estimated}
Assume a common scale family indexed by the design variance (A1),
anti-concentration of the sup-score law near its upper quantile (A2), and
bounded curves with an unbiased size-$B$ design bootstrap (A3); the appendix
states all three precisely.
Then \emph{(i)} with oracle scales the deconvolution band is finite-sample
\emph{exact} for the latent target at every $K$, and \emph{(ii)} with estimated
scales it satisfies
$\Pr(F_{\text{new}}\in\widehat B)\ge 1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{K,B}$ with
$\varepsilon_{K,B}=O\big(\sqrt{\log(KT)/\min(K,B)}\,\big)$, proved by a sandwich and
concentration argument with constants depending only on the constants of
(A1)--(A3). The finite-$K$-safe scale
$\widehat s_T^2$ (Section~\ref{sec:method}) subtracts a lower confidence bound on the
design variance, making the scale-error term one-sided (wide, not narrow) outside an
$\alpha_2$-probability event. Some shape restriction is not removable---the bimodal counterexample of
Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b) defeats any latent-target band without one---and (A1)
is one sufficient choice, strong enough to make the distributional matching
exact.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Safe-adaptive finite-$K$ validity---the deployed pipeline]
\label{thm:safe}
Deploy Algorithm~\ref{alg:safe} with unstudentized PCB and conservative branches
(scores $\max_t|E_c(t)|$ and $\max_t(|E_c(t)|+z\widehat v_c(t))$, both at level
$\alpha_{\text{anchor}}$) and the deconvolution branch at its own budget
$\alpha_{\text{dec}}$, where $(\alpha_{\text{anchor}},\alpha_{\text{dec}})=(\alpha,0)$
when the reliability gate is infeasible at the observed $K$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:unreach}: $K<94$, which covers every certification sample
analyzed in this paper, including the WVS $\ge2$-wave sets at $K=59$--$77$) and
otherwise $\alpha_{\text{dec}}=\min\{\max(0.1\alpha,\,3/(K{+}1)),\,\alpha/2\}$ with
$\alpha_{\text{anchor}}=\alpha-\alpha_{\text{dec}}$, a frozen function of $K$ alone.
Then, finite-sample and with \emph{no conditions on the selection rule},
\[
\Pr\big(\Ftilde_{\text{new},r}(t)\in\widehat B\big)\ge1-\alpha\ \ (K<94),
\qquad
\Pr\big(F_{\text{new},r}(t)\in\widehat B\big)\ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{K,B}\ \
(K\ge94).
\]
The clauses name different targets because they rest on different assumptions:
exchangeability alone gives the estimated trajectory T1 exactly (the latent T2
adding Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}(b)'s gap), while (A1)--(A3)---already inherited
from Theorem~\ref{thm:estimated} through its remainder $\varepsilon_{K,B}$, which
is attached to the deconvolution branch only---additionally deliver the latent
target directly, by an anchor-domination lemma (Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}).
\end{theorem}

The proof uses no conditional-on-selection step: a nested-band lemma (the
conservative score dominates the PCB score pointwise, and selection between
nested bands anchored at an exact band is validity-free---an instance of
nested-conformal machinery \citep{gupta2022nested,yang2024selection}, our
contribution being to engineer the branches so it applies) plus a union bound
charging the non-nested branch its own budget where its gate can open. Two scope
notes: at $K\ge94$ the \emph{reported} level
$1-\alpha-\widehat\delta_{\text{UCB}}(D)$ is a frozen calibrated bound, not a
theorem; and the unsurveyed-target mode with leave-one-out centers carries a
residual $O(1/K^2)$ asymmetry bounded only heuristically, so a strict guarantee
there wants a split-fold center.

\paragraph{Efficiency.} \emph{(AW-1, proved)} the low-$\rho$ deconvolution/PCB
width ratio is $1-\tfrac12\rho^2+O(\rho^4)$; \emph{(AW-2)} conservative
$\supseteq$ PCB holds pointwise, its Bonferroni domination resting on an unproved
expansion; \emph{(AW-3, proved)} selection cannot inflate miscoverage
(Theorem~\ref{thm:safe}). AW-4 (open) and the fitted $O(g/K)$ modulation deficit
are in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.
