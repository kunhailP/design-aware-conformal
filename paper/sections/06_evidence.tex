\section{Evidence from the ESS and the AmericasBarometer}\label{sec:evidence}

\paragraph{The regime characterization.} On real cross-national data the honest band
reduces to clustered conformal---and the reason is instructive. Across \emph{both}
surveys, \emph{both} estimands (attitude levels and
wave-to-wave change), and three outcomes (trust in parliament, satisfaction with
democracy, system support), national cross-national inference is a uniformly
\emph{low design-noise} regime: the estimated $\rhohat\le0.20$ (LAPOP, shown in
Figure~\ref{fig:rho}; the ESS subgroup scan reaches at most $0.29$, reported below),
comfortably below the $\rho_0=0.47$ cutoff, because between-country variation dwarfs
within-survey design noise. Two directional caveats attach to every $\rhohat$ we
report: the PSU bootstrap resamples $m$-of-$m$ without the Rao--Wu--Yue rescaling
\citep{rao1992recent}, and single-PSU strata contribute no variance, so design
variance---hence $\rhohat$---is biased \emph{downward} by an unquantified amount;
the qualitative regime conclusion survives because the observed gap to $\rho_0$ is
severalfold, but the numbers are floors, not measurements. Consequently the safe
selector reduces to clustered PCB on \emph{all} real data---not by assumption but by
its own low-$\rho$ gate. The elaborate design-aware machinery is, on real
cross-national attitude data, correctly inactive. \begin{figure}[t]\centering
\includegraphics[width=.7\textwidth]{lapop_level_vs_change_rho.png}
\caption{Estimated design-to-total SD ratio $\rhohat$ for the AmericasBarometer,
levels and wave-to-wave change. Every country-outcome sits well below the $\rho_0=0.47$
cutoff: national cross-national attitude inference is a uniformly low design-noise
regime, so the safe selector reduces to clustered PCB.}\label{fig:rho}
\end{figure}

This is a substantive finding: the impossibility of Section~\ref{sec:setup} bites in
principle, but the deconvolution it necessitates is never triggered at survey scale, so
honest inference here \emph{is} clustered conformal on the observed scores.

\paragraph{Why the deconvolution is unreachable, precisely.} The inactivity is not an
accident of one dataset; it is forced by two structural barriers, and we can say how far
each real survey is from clearing them.

\begin{proposition}[Survey-scale unreachability]\label{prop:unreach}
The deployed selector activates the deconvolution branch only if both a \emph{need} gate
$\rhohat_{\mathrm{LCB}}>\rho_0$ and a \emph{reliability} gate $D\le\tau_D$ hold, where
$D=\max_t \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\widehat s_T^2)/\widehat s_T^2$. Two facts bound
these, both \emph{algorithmic identities of the frozen procedure}---they hold for
every possible dataset, not on average (Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} separates this
deterministic statement from its kurtosis-dependent statistical interpretation).
\emph{(a)} $\rho=\sqrt{\overline{v^2}}/s_{\mathrm{plug}}\in[0,1)$ with
$s_{\mathrm{plug}}^2=s_R^2+\overline{v^2}$, so the need gate can fire only when the design
noise is an appreciable fraction of the between-population signal $s_R$. \emph{(b)} Since
$\widehat s_T\le s_{\mathrm{plug}}$ by construction, the reliability obeys
$D\ge\sqrt{2/(K-1)}$; hence the reliability gate requires
$K\ge 1+2/\tau_D^2$, i.e.\ $K\ge94$ at the frozen $\tau_D=0.147$, exchangeable
populations.
\end{proposition}

The two requirements pull apart on real surveys (Figure~\ref{fig:unreach}). On the ESS,
narrowing to the noisiest subpopulations (youth age bands) raises $\rhohat$ from $0.08$
to at most $0.29$---still far below $\rho_0$---while $K\le33$ leaves $D\ge0.25$, so
\emph{neither} gate opens (a scan of $42$ subpopulation cells activates deconvolution in
none). The World Values Survey is the mirror image, with one denominator made
explicit: the gate probe runs on the \emph{full item-coverage} sets ($K=95$--$105$
country-level units per item), where the reliability gate is feasible (the floor
$\sqrt{2/(K-1)}$ sits below $\tau_D=0.147$; four of the five items measure
$D=0.139$--$0.142$ and pass, while the importance-of-democracy item sits at
$D=0.1473$, a hair's breadth over the frozen threshold). The \emph{certification}
samples of Section~\ref{sec:political}---countries with $\ge2$ waves, $K=59$--$77$
per item---sit below $K=94$, where the floor ($0.16$--$0.19$) closes the gate
outright. And in either denominator the WVS ships no cluster identifiers, so its
weights-only design noise gives $\rhohat_{\mathrm{LCB}}\le0.09$---itself biased
downward by the missing clustering---and the need gate fails decisively. The survey large enough to \emph{estimate} the deconvolution has almost no
design noise to remove; the survey with real clustered design noise has far too few
countries to estimate it. The two barriers are anti-correlated, and neither of the two
largest repeated cross-national surveys clears both.

\begin{figure}[t]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{unreachability.png}
\caption{The two barriers, measured on real data. \emph{Left:} across ESS age-band
subpopulations $\rhohat$ stays far below $\rho_0=0.47$ (max $0.29$; the estimates are
downward-biased floors, Section text). \emph{Right:} the finite-$K$
reliability $D$ sits on its algorithmic floor $\sqrt{2/(K-1)}$ (deterministic for the

\paragraph{The barriers are not survey artifacts.} Both gates are distribution-free, so
the boundary transfers to any conformal procedure calibrated on estimated objects
(Remark~\ref{rem:general}); a non-survey MRP-style simulation confirms the reliability
floor and identifies the one many-area setting, with appreciable posterior uncertainty,
where the \emph{need} gate can fire (Supplementary Material).

\paragraph{The AmericasBarometer's merged design metadata.} Its full
stratified-PSU structure ships in one merged file (the ESS's equivalent lives in
per-round sample-design files, integrated only from round 9---a data-handling
constraint on our window, not an ESS limitation), letting us compute a proper design
bootstrap, and the design
effect is real (median $\approx1.13$--$1.20$, up to $1.9$ in the most clustered
country-years). Yet certification is coarse-robust: the design effect moves band
\emph{width} but rarely flips the binary decline decision, because the decision is
dominated by the between-country transport spread, not the within-country design
noise---the same low-$\rho$ fact, seen from the certification side (details in the
Supplementary Material).

\paragraph{What real data validates, and what it cannot.} Real data validates the
reduction to PCB, the target-blind selection, the efficiency of PCB over the
conservative envelope, the real design effect, and the political reclassification of
Section~\ref{sec:political}. It \emph{cannot} validate the high-$\rho$ deconvolution
branch or the finite-$K$ remainder---those regimes do not occur at survey scale and
rest on the simulation of Section~\ref{sec:simulation} and the theorems, not on the
ESS and AmericasBarometer data.
