\section{Evidence from the ESS and the AmericasBarometer}\label{sec:evidence}

\paragraph{The regime characterization.} On real data the selector reduces to
the clustered conformal band. Across both surveys, both estimands, and three
outcomes, cross-national inference is a uniformly \emph{low design-noise}
regime: $\rhohat\le0.22$ on the
AmericasBarometer, at most $0.29$ in the ESS subgroup scan, well below the
$\rho_0=0.47$ cutoff. Every $\rhohat$ we report is a downward-biased
\emph{floor}, because the PSU bootstrap resamples $m$-of-$m$ without
Rao--Wu--Yue rescaling \citep{rao1992recent}; the rescaled rerun confirms the
floors are tight --- maxima $0.28$ and $0.22$, still severalfold below
$\rho_0$, zero gate activations, every certification count unchanged
(Supplementary Material). The reduction is not an assumption but the
selector's own low-$\rho$ gate at work.

\paragraph{Why the deconvolution is unreachable, precisely.}

\begin{proposition}[Finite-$K$ feasibility of the deployed correction]\label{prop:unreach}
The deployed selector activates deconvolution only if a \emph{need} gate
$\rhohat_{\mathrm{LCB}}>\rho_0$ and a \emph{reliability} gate $D\le\tau_D$ both
hold, $D=\max_t \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\widehat s_T^2)/\widehat s_T^2$. Both are
bounded by \emph{algorithmic identities of the frozen procedure}.
(a) $\rho=\sqrt{\overline{v^2}}/s_{\mathrm{plug}}\in[0,1)$ with
$s_{\mathrm{plug}}^2=s_R^2+\overline{v^2}$: the need gate fires only when design
noise is an appreciable fraction of the between-population signal.
(b) Since $\widehat s_T\le s_{\mathrm{plug}}$ by construction,
$D\ge\sqrt{2/(K-1)}$, so the reliability gate requires $K\ge1+2/\tau_D^2$, i.e.\
$K\ge94$ at the frozen $\tau_D=0.147$. (the Supplementary Material separates the
deterministic statement from its kurtosis-dependent interpretation.)
\end{proposition}

The two requirements pull apart on real surveys (Figure~\ref{fig:frontier}). On
the ESS the noisiest subpopulations raise $\rhohat$ only to $0.29$ while $K\le33$
leaves $D\ge0.25$: neither gate opens ($42$ cells, zero activations). The
WVS is the mirror image: its full item-coverage sets ($K=95$--$105$) make the
reliability gate feasible, the certification samples ($K=59$--$77$) do not, and
in either denominator the weights-only $\rhohat_{\mathrm{LCB}}\le0.09$ fails the
need gate decisively. The survey large enough to estimate the deconvolution has
almost no design noise to remove; the survey with real clustered noise has far
too few countries.

Nor is the floor an artifact of our thresholds. Any \emph{unbiased} estimator
of a variance from $K$ exchangeable populations has relative standard error at
least $\sqrt{2/(K-1)}$ at the Gaussian member (Lehmann--Scheff\'e;
Supplementary Material), so any correction relying on such an estimate at
relative reliability $\tau$ --- the class the deployed diagnostic lives in ---
needs $K\ge1+2/\tau^2$: the frozen $\tau_D$ is one instantiation, $94$ its
intercept, $\sqrt{2/(K-1)}$ the law for this estimator class. With the proved
width gain $1-\sqrt{1-\rho^2}$, the $(K,\rho)$ plane splits into three
regimes --- correction \emph{unnecessary}, \emph{unlearnable},
\emph{feasible} --- and this paper's datasets occupy the three corners
(Figure~\ref{fig:frontier}).

\paragraph{Where the correction does live: small areas, on this same survey.}
The barrier is about the unit, and the ESS itself contains a finer one. Take the
estimand this regime is actually for, a region's departure from its own
\emph{national} distribution, $D_g=F_g-F_{\text{nation}}$, and calibrate the
transport band across regions, with the design SD of the difference from a
single stratified-PSU bootstrap of the whole country (construction and
exclusion rules in the Supplementary Material). On that estimand the
design-to-total ratio is not marginal: $\rhohat_{\text{LCB}}$ runs
$0.26$--$0.52$ and clears $\rho_0=0.47$ in eight of twenty-three unit-rounds.
Reliability needs scale, and the two gates open together only once
$K\gtrsim230$: units of forty to sixty respondents, rounds 9 and 10,
$K=228$--$287$. There the \emph{frozen} selector, no constant retuned,
activates the design-aware branch for the first time on real survey data: a
band $21$--$27\%$ narrower than the conservative envelope at the reported
level $0.88$, with certified width-gain lower bounds of $0.16$--$0.22$ across
twelve independent bootstrap streams.

The regions nest in twenty-seven countries, nine each, so the objection this
paper would itself raise is that the effective unit is the country and $K$ is
really $27$. Holding out whole countries provides the diagnostic: calibrating
on the other twenty-six countries' regions leaves the plug-in anchor at
$0.89$--$0.90$ against its $0.90$ target in all twelve cells, within $0.015$
of leave-one-region-out calibration --- deleting nine country-mates changes
essentially nothing, evidence against within-country dependence deflating the
quantile. Coverage \emph{conditional} on the country does not transfer (the
worst held-out countries sit at $0.53$--$0.63$; Supplementary Material); the
guarantee is marginal over regions, as conformal guarantees are. In the
activating cells the selected band covers $0.94$--$0.98$ against its nominal
$0.88$, scored on the observed departure; the latent departure is
unobservable, so we read the activation as feasibility --- the frozen
procedure enters its correction regime at this unit --- with latent-target
validity resting on the scale-family condition (A1) as everywhere in that
branch. Two scope conditions travel with the result: reaching $K\gtrsim230$
requires pooling regions coded at different NUTS levels (restricted to the
fourteen countries sharing one level, the need gate still opens but
$K\le140$ keeps the reliability gate shut), and round 11 never activates,
its regional design noise being lower. The correction is thus not unreachable
in principle, and not reachable across countries: it lives at the small-area
scale, where Theorem~\ref{thm:impossible}'s remark said it would.

\paragraph{How coarse can the calibration unit be?} One constraint is sharp
and, we believe, unremarked: the conformal level is infeasible whenever
$K<\alpha^{-1}-1$, the required order statistic exceeding the sample --- a
too-coarse unit is unusable, not merely inefficient; and refining is not free
either, half-width falling threefold as units coarsen over the observed range
(Supplementary Material). Both are statements about the \emph{calibration}
unit; an estimand's unit is fixed by the question, and choosing among
substantive areal partitions is the modifiable areal unit problem
\citep{openshaw1984modifiable,robinson1950ecological}, to which we add
nothing.
