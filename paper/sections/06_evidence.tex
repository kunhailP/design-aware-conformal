\section{Evidence from the ESS and the AmericasBarometer}\label{sec:evidence}

\paragraph{The regime characterization.} On real data the honest band reduces to
clustered conformal---instructively. Across both surveys, both estimands, and
three outcomes, cross-national inference is a uniformly \emph{low design-noise}
regime:
$\rhohat\le0.22$ on LAPOP, at most $0.29$ in the ESS
subgroup scan, well below the $\rho_0=0.47$ cutoff. Two directional caveats attach
to every $\rhohat$ we report: the PSU bootstrap resamples $m$-of-$m$ without
Rao--Wu--Yue rescaling \citep{rao1992recent} and single-PSU strata contribute no
variance, so the estimates are downward-biased \emph{floors}. The Rao--Wu--Yue rescaled rerun confirms the floors are tight: maxima $0.281$
(ESS) and $0.222$ (LAPOP), still severalfold below $\rho_0$, zero gate
activations, every certification count unchanged; and singleton strata, which no
rescaling repairs, are $1$ of $7{,}028$ in the core rounds. The safe selector
thus reduces to clustered PCB on all real data---not by assumption but by its own
low-$\rho$ gate.

\paragraph{Why the deconvolution is unreachable, precisely.}

\begin{proposition}[Survey-scale unreachability]\label{prop:unreach}
The deployed selector activates deconvolution only if a \emph{need} gate
$\rhohat_{\mathrm{LCB}}>\rho_0$ and a \emph{reliability} gate $D\le\tau_D$ both
hold, $D=\max_t \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\widehat s_T^2)/\widehat s_T^2$. Both are
bounded by \emph{algorithmic identities of the frozen procedure}.
\emph{(a)} $\rho=\sqrt{\overline{v^2}}/s_{\mathrm{plug}}\in[0,1)$ with
$s_{\mathrm{plug}}^2=s_R^2+\overline{v^2}$: the need gate fires only when design
noise is an appreciable fraction of the between-population signal.
\emph{(b)} Since $\widehat s_T\le s_{\mathrm{plug}}$ by construction,
$D\ge\sqrt{2/(K-1)}$, so the reliability gate requires $K\ge1+2/\tau_D^2$, i.e.\
$K\ge94$ at the frozen $\tau_D=0.147$. (Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} separates the
deterministic statement from its kurtosis-dependent interpretation.)
\end{proposition}

The two requirements pull apart on real surveys (Figure~\ref{fig:unreach}). On
the ESS the noisiest subpopulations raise $\rhohat$ only to $0.29$ while $K\le33$
leaves $D\ge0.25$: \emph{neither} gate opens ($42$ cells, zero activations). The
WVS is the mirror image---its full item-coverage sets ($K=95$--$105$) make the
reliability gate feasible, but the certification samples ($K=59$--$77$) sit below
$94$, and in either denominator the weights-only
$\rhohat_{\mathrm{LCB}}\le0.09$ fails the need gate decisively. The survey large
enough to estimate the deconvolution has almost no design noise to remove; the
survey with real clustered noise has far too few countries.

\paragraph{Where the correction does live: small areas, on this same survey.}
The barrier is about the unit, and the ESS itself contains a finer one. Take the
estimand this regime is actually for---a region's departure from its own
\emph{national} distribution, $D_g=F_g-F_{\text{nation}}$---and calibrate the
transport band across regions (\texttt{e54}). A single stratified-PSU bootstrap
of the whole country recomputes $F_g$ and $F_{\text{nation}}$ on every replicate,
so the design SD is that of the difference; the missing-value sentinel and the
three countries whose ``region'' is the country are excluded, and a country must
contribute at least two regions to express a departure at all.

On that estimand the design-to-total ratio is not marginal: $\rhohat_{\text{LCB}}$
runs $0.26$--$0.52$ and clears $\rho_0=0.47$ in eight of twenty-three
unit-rounds. Reliability needs scale, and the two open together only once
$K\gtrsim230$---units of forty to sixty respondents, rounds 9 and 10,
$K=228$--$287$---where the \emph{frozen} selector, no constant retuned,
activates the design-aware branch for the first time on real survey data: a band
$15$--$17\%$ narrower than the conservative envelope at the reported level
$0.881$, clearing the efficiency gate by $0.14$--$0.22$ against its $0.10$
threshold, and reproduced across twelve independent bootstrap streams.

Two scope conditions travel with it. Reaching $K\gtrsim230$ requires pooling
regions coded at different NUTS levels; restricted to the fourteen countries
sharing one level the need gate still opens in nine of twenty-one unit-rounds,
but $K\le140$ keeps the reliability gate shut and the selector falls back to the
conservative envelope---the same two-barrier structure as Section~\ref{sec:evidence}'s
national result, one level down. And round 11 never activates, because its
regional design noise is lower. The correction is therefore not unreachable in
principle, and not reachable across countries: it lives at the small-area scale,
which is where Theorem~\ref{thm:impossible}'s remark said it would.

\paragraph{How coarse can the calibration unit be?} One constraint on that
choice is sharp and, as far as we know, unremarked: the conformal level is
\emph{infeasible} whenever $K<\alpha^{-1}-1$, because the required order
statistic exceeds the sample and the band is $[0,1]$ at any width. A too-coarse
unit is therefore not inefficient but unusable, and the cross-national default
clears the floor comfortably ($K=30$--$34$ against nine). Refining below what the
floor demands is not free either: building units of a controlled target size by
grouping whole PSUs within each country---so size is set rather than filtered and
every country contributes at every size---the band's half-width falls
\emph{monotonically} as units coarsen, from $0.190$ at forty respondents per unit
to $0.065$ at three hundred and fifty (\texttt{e52}). These are statements about
the \emph{calibration} unit of a transport band; the unit of an estimand is fixed
by the question, and choosing among substantive areal partitions is the
modifiable areal unit problem \citep{openshaw1984modifiable,robinson1950ecological},
to which we add nothing.
