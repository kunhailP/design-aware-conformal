\section{Evidence from the ESS and the AmericasBarometer}\label{sec:evidence}

\paragraph{The regime characterization.} On real cross-national data the honest band
reduces to clustered conformal---instructively. Across both surveys, both estimands
(levels and change), and three outcomes, national cross-national inference is a
uniformly \emph{low design-noise} regime:
$\rhohat\le0.22$ on LAPOP (Figure~\ref{fig:rho}), at most $0.29$ in the ESS
subgroup scan, well below the $\rho_0=0.47$ cutoff. Two directional caveats attach
to every $\rhohat$ we report: the PSU bootstrap resamples $m$-of-$m$ without
Rao--Wu--Yue rescaling \citep{rao1992recent} and single-PSU strata contribute no
variance, so the estimates are downward-biased \emph{floors}; the qualitative
conclusion survives because the gap to $\rho_0$ is severalfold. The safe selector
thus reduces to clustered PCB on all real data---not by assumption but by its own
low-$\rho$ gate.

\includegraphics[width=.7\textwidth]{lapop_level_vs_change_rho.png}
\caption{Design-to-total SD ratio $\rhohat$, AmericasBarometer (levels and change;
$B=200$ design bootstrap). All country-outcomes sit well below $\rho_0=0.47$;

\end{figure}

\paragraph{Why the deconvolution is unreachable, precisely.}

\begin{proposition}[Survey-scale unreachability]\label{prop:unreach}
The deployed selector activates the deconvolution branch only if a \emph{need} gate
$\rhohat_{\mathrm{LCB}}>\rho_0$ and a \emph{reliability} gate $D\le\tau_D$ both hold,
with $D=\max_t \widehat{\mathrm{SE}}(\widehat s_T^2)/\widehat s_T^2$. Two facts bound
these, both \emph{algorithmic identities of the frozen procedure}
(Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} separates this deterministic statement from its
kurtosis-dependent statistical interpretation).
\emph{(a)} $\rho=\sqrt{\overline{v^2}}/s_{\mathrm{plug}}\in[0,1)$ with
$s_{\mathrm{plug}}^2=s_R^2+\overline{v^2}$: the need gate fires only when design
noise is an appreciable fraction of the between-population signal. \emph{(b)} Since
$\widehat s_T\le s_{\mathrm{plug}}$ by construction, $D\ge\sqrt{2/(K-1)}$; the
reliability gate requires $K\ge 1+2/\tau_D^2$, i.e.\ $K\ge94$ at the frozen
$\tau_D=0.147$.
\end{proposition}

The two requirements pull apart on real surveys (Figure~\ref{fig:unreach}). On the
ESS, the noisiest subpopulations (youth age bands) raise $\rhohat$ only to $0.29$,
while $K\le33$ leaves $D\ge0.25$: \emph{neither} gate opens ($42$ cells scanned,
zero activations). The WVS is the mirror image, with one denominator made explicit:
its \emph{full item-coverage} sets ($K=95$--$105$) make the reliability gate
feasible (four items pass at $D=0.139$--$0.142$; importance-of-democracy misses at
$0.1473$), but the \emph{certification} samples of Section~\ref{sec:political}
($\ge2$ waves, $K=59$--$77$) sit below $94$, where the floor closes the gate
outright---and in either denominator its weights-only $\rhohat_{\mathrm{LCB}}\le
0.09$ (itself biased low by the missing clustering) fails the need gate decisively.
The survey large enough to estimate the deconvolution has almost no design noise to
remove; the survey with real clustered design noise has far too few countries. The
barriers transfer beyond surveys; the one many-unit setting where the need gate
fires (MRP-style small areas) is demonstrated in the Supplementary Material, along
with the AmericasBarometer's design-effect audit (median deff $1.14$--$1.21$, up to
$2.0$).
